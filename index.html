The Story

Picture it.
First Friday.
A Tulsa hotel bar.

It's 6 o'clock on a first Friday. The drinks are cold, the crowd is fabulous, and somewhere across the room someone is laughing at something so good you have to walk over and introduce yourself.

That's 4H: Homo Hotel Happy Hour. Every first Friday of the month, we take over a different Tulsa hotel bar for two hours of cocktails, conversation, and community. No cover charge. No dress code. No drama. Just the finest, most fun, most fabulously diverse LGBTQIA+ crowd in Tulsa doing what we do best.

And while we're all having the time of our lives, we pass the hat for a local charity. Because being fabulous and being generous aren't mutually exclusive. Never have been.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need before your first First Friday.

What is Homo Hotel Happy Hour?

Homo Hotel Happy Hour (4H) is Tulsa's free monthly LGBTQIA+ cocktail happy hour. On the first Friday of every month we take over a different Tulsa hotel bar, raise money for a local charity with an all-night raffle, and welcome everyone from regulars to first-timers. No cover, no dress code.

When and where is the next Homo Hotel Happy Hour?

4H happens on the first Friday of every month at a rotating Tulsa hotel bar, with one exception: September 2026 moves to Friday, September 11 (venue still to be announced). The upcoming venue and date are always posted at the top of this page and on our Meetup. RSVP on Meetup or join our Facebook group to get the details each month.

Is it really free?

Yes. Admission is always free, with no cover and no dress code. The only money involved is the optional charity raffle, and nearly all of those proceeds go straight to the featured local Tulsa cause.

What charities does 4H support?

Every event raises money for a local Tulsa charity through the raffle and bucket donations. Past beneficiaries include Council Oak Men's Chorale and Queer Lit Collective. Nearly all raffle proceeds go directly to the featured charity; we cover only minimal production costs like our photographer.

How do I attend or get involved?

Just show up on the first Friday, or RSVP on Meetup and join our Facebook group so you never miss a venue announcement. Want to nominate a charity or co-host? Message us on Instagram @homohotelhappyhour.

Ready to Join the Party?

No cover. No dress code. Just great drinks, a great crowd, and a great cause. First Friday of every month, Tulsa.
